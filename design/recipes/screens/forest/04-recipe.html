💍🐣 Marry Me Chicken

Linnea’s idea · Recipe
Prep10 min
Cook30 min
Total40 min
ServingsAmounts change, times don’t 4

Ingredients

In your kitchen8 of 11
1½ lb boneless chicken thighs
¼ cup all-purpose flour
2 tbsp olive oil
4 cloves garlic, minced
½ cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
1 cup chicken broth
¾ cup heavy cream
½ cup grated parmesan
1 tsp dried thyme
½ tsp red pepper flakes
fresh basil, to serve

Steps 6

  1. 1 Pat the chicken dry, season it with salt and pepper, and dust it with the flour.
  2. 2 Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Sear the chicken until golden, about 5 minutes a side, then move it to a plate. 5 min timer
  3. 3 Lower the heat to medium. Add the garlic and sun-dried tomatoes and cook until fragrant, about 2 minutes. 2 min timer
  4. 4 Pour in the broth and cream, add the thyme and pepper flakes, and bring it to a gentle simmer.
  5. 5 Stir in the parmesan, return the chicken, and simmer until the sauce thickens and the chicken is cooked through, about 10 minutes. 10 min timer
  6. 6 Tear the basil over the top and serve.
